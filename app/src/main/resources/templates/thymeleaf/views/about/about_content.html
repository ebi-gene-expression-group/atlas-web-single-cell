Single Cell Expression Atlas is moving to FENIX Explorer

Privacy Policy

We are developing the Functional Genomics Index, FENIX, as a unified platform for discovering, exploring and, in future, contributing functional genomics data at EMBL-EBI. Its first public component, FENIX Explorer, will replace the data exploration interface currently provided by Single Cell Expression Atlas (SCEA).

We plan to launch FENIX Explorer in public beta in November 2026. SCEA will remain available while eligible datasets are migrated and validated and its retirement date will be announced in advance on this page.

What you can do in FENIX Explorer

FENIX Explorer combines metadata and gene search with interactive visualisation powered by Vitessce. Four dashboard views allow you to explore individual genes, examine several genes together, view expression patterns in a heatmap and explore cells in a UMAP embedding.

The beta will include prioritised single-cell RNA-seq datasets from SCEA alongside compatible datasets available through CELLxGENE. Spatial data exploration is planned for a subsequent release early 2027.

What happens to existing SCEA datasets?

We are migrating SCEA datasets in phases. For the beta, we are prioritising datasets from species not currently represented in CELLxGENE, alongside some of the most frequently accessed SCEA datasets. Other datasets will follow after launch, subject to necessary schema extensions for some species.

FENIX Explorer requires cell type annotations for interactive exploration. SCEA datasets without cell type labels will therefore not be migrated. If you generated one of these datasets and would like to explore how it could be included, please contact our helpdesk. The raw data for these datasets will remain available from the archives where they were deposited, under those archives’ access arrangements. SCEA-generated processed outputs are currently available through our FTP service. We will publish arrangements for access after retirement, including any retention limits, on this page before SCEA closes.

Adopting the scFAIR standard

We are adopting the scFAIR community standard to improve interoperability between single-cell data resources. As part of the scFAIR community, we are adapting our processing pipelines and outputs so that eligible SCEA datasets can be explored in FENIX Explorer.

A shared standard also helps us include compatible datasets prepared by other resources. The beta will include selected datasets available through CELLxGENE, and we aim to extend coverage to other compatible sources, including Bgee. Dataset provenance will identify the source and processing information relevant to each resource.

Contributing compatible datasets?

Researchers interested in making a compatible dataset available through FENIX Explorer can contact our helpdesk to discuss inclusion. The ASAP compliance tool can help check a dataset against the scFAIR schema and identify changes needed for compatibility.

The beta does not include a self-service submission interface. Please continue to deposit underlying raw data through the appropriate archive (e.g. submitting through Annotare) and contact us about making the processed dataset available for exploration.

The wider FENIX programme

Future phases of FENIX will improve the pathways through which functional genomics data are submitted, archived and made available for exploration. Our aim is to connect raw-data archiving, structured metadata and interactive visualisation more closely, creating comprehensive, reusable records and helping researchers present datasets in the context intended by their authors.

The wider FENIX project is supported by the Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council through its Bioinformatics and Biological Resources Fund (BBSRC BBR), alongside support from Wellcome for the development of the data exploration interface.
