How to cite Expression Atlas

If you’re using Expression Atlas in your research, please consider citing our most recent update in Nucleic Acids Research:

Expression Atlas in 2026: enabling FAIR and open expression data through community collaboration and integration
Nucleic Acids Research, 10 December 2025.

If you would like to reference specific historical updates or methodology, you can also cite:

Expression Atlas update: insights from sequencing data at both bulk and single cell level
Nucleic Acids Research, 22 November 2023.

Expression Atlas update: gene and protein expression in multiple species
Nucleic Acids Research, 24 November 2021.

User-friendly, scalable tools and workflows for single-cell RNA-seq analysis
Nature Methods, 29 March 2021.
